Questions

Answers before you call.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. E&E carries a Colorado electrical license and liability insurance, and we're happy to show proof of both before any work starts.

Do you give free estimates?

Yes, for jobs in our service area. Text us a photo and a short description and we can often give you a ballpark the same day. Bigger jobs like remodels get an on-site walkthrough and a written estimate.

Do you pull permits for electrical work?

Yes. When a job needs a permit, like a panel upgrade or new circuits, we pull it and schedule the inspection, so the work is on record and done to code.

Can you come out for urgent problems?

Yes. We take emergency calls, even outside our regular hours (Monday to Friday, 7am to 5pm). For things like a dead circuit, a tree limb on the roof or storm damage, call us and we'll get to you as fast as we can. If you smell burning or see sparks, turn off the breaker and call 911 first.

Do you work with landlords and property managers?

Yes. We handle rental turnovers, make-readies and ongoing upkeep, and one call covers electrical, repairs, trees and concrete across all your properties.

What areas do you serve?

Colorado Springs and the rest of El Paso County, including Fountain, Monument, Falcon, Security-Widefield, Manitou Springs and Peyton. If you're nearby and not on the list, just ask.

Do you clean up when the job is done?

Always. Drop cloths go down before we start, and debris, branches and old concrete are hauled away when we finish.
