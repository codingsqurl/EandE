Common questions.

Estimates, hours, permits and where we work. Don't see your question? Call or text and ask.

Call (720) 838-1503 Text a photo
Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. E&E carries a Colorado electrical license and liability insurance, and we're happy to show proof of both before any work starts.

Do you give free estimates?

Yes, estimates are free. Repairs start with a $150 diagnostic visit to find the problem, and that $150 goes toward the final price. Text us a photo and a short description and we can often give you a ballpark the same day. Bigger jobs like remodels get an on-site walkthrough and a written estimate.

Do you pull permits for electrical work?

Yes. When a job needs a permit, like a panel upgrade or new circuits, we pull it and schedule the inspection, so the work is on record and done to code.

Can you come out for urgent problems?

Yes. We take emergency calls, even outside our regular hours (Monday to Friday, 7am to 5pm Mountain Time). For things like a dead circuit, a tree limb on the roof or storm damage, call us and we'll get to you as fast as we can. If you smell burning or see sparks, turn off the breaker and call 911 first.

Do you work with landlords and property managers?

Yes. We handle rental turnovers, make-readies and ongoing upkeep, and one call covers electrical, repairs, trees and concrete across all your properties.

What areas do you serve?

Colorado Springs, the rest of El Paso County and every mountain town between the Springs and Aspen, including Woodland Park, Divide, Florissant, Lake George, Buena Vista and Twin Lakes. If you're nearby and not on the list, just ask.

Do you clean up when the job is done?

Always. Drop cloths go down before we start, and debris, branches and old concrete are hauled away when we finish.

Tell us about the job.

Call, text or email for a free estimate. A photo of the job helps us give you a quick, honest price.
