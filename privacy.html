Privacy.

What this website collects, and how to say no.

The short version

This site doesn't ask for your name, address or payment details, and it has no forms. When you call or text us, that goes straight to our phone, not through the website.

Google Analytics, only if you accept

If you press Accept on the cookie banner, the site uses Google Analytics to count visits and see how people find and use it, so we can make it better. Google then sets cookies in your browser and receives details like the pages you view, how long you stay, the site or search that sent you, your device and browser type, and your rough location (city or region, worked out from your internet address, which Google Analytics doesn't store in full). It's used for counting only: nothing is shared for advertising.

If you press Decline, or never answer, Google Analytics doesn't load at all and sets no cookies. Your choice is saved in your browser so the banner doesn't keep asking.

Google's own policy explains how it handles this data: policies.google.com/privacy.

Google Maps on the service area pages

The service area page and each town's page show a Google map. To draw it, your browser connects to Google, which receives your internet address and the name of this site, and may set its own cookies, as it does on any site that shows a Google map. This happens whatever you choose on the cookie banner. Google's policy above covers it. The other pages don't show a map.

Server logs

Like almost every website, the server notes each request it answers: your internet address, the page asked for, and the time. That's used only to keep the site running and to block attacks, never to identify visitors or for advertising.

Changing your mind

Use Cookie settings at the bottom of any page to bring the banner back. Switching to Decline stops Google Analytics, reloads the page without it, and deletes its cookies from this site. Clearing your browser's data for this site does the same.
